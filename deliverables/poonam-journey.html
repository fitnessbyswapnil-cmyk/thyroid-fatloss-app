One marker tested. Three missing.

TSH is the signal your brain sends. It doesn't show how much hormone your body actually converts and uses, and it doesn't show whether your immune system is involved. Vitamin D, B12, ferritin and fasting insulin aren't there either — and every one of them affects how you feel in the morning.

Nobody is going to know what those say until they're measured. That's the first thing we do, and it's why the plan starts with a conversation and a test, not a diet chart.

The Method

Three pillars, working
together or not at all.

01

Fix the root

Your weight won't move despite trying multiple diets, and you feel constantly low on energy. That's because the root — your thyroid and hormonal health — hasn't been addressed. It's like pressing the accelerator with the handbrake on. So we track your markers, understand the gaps in your day, and use hormone-supportive nutrition and routine to reset your metabolism. Your body starts responding again, and stops feeling like it's working against you.

02

Fuel the body

You're tired of complicated diets that cut out Indian food and leave you feeling deprived and guilty. So the second pillar is about fuelling the body, not starving it — simple Indian home-cooked meals built to support thyroid healing. You eat smarter, feel more energetic, and still enjoy your food. It's like charging your phone properly instead of running it on 5% all day.

03

Flow into fitness

You don't have the energy or time for long workouts, and your joints hurt if you push. So we take a low-impact, lifestyle-friendly approach: joint-friendly routines, home or gym, gradual progression. We build your sleep and daily rhythm alongside it, so you naturally feel stronger and more consistent instead of forcing it.

These three work in sync. If one isn't properly implemented, the strategy doesn't hold. So to help you execute it, five things come with it — they're on the last page.
